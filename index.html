Laboratory Rules

Help us keep the laboratory safe, clean, and available to everyone.

    USING OF MOBILE PHONE DEVICES IS PROHIBITED

  1. Use laboratory computers only for authorized academic or institutional activities.
  2. The student should wear a proper uniform and ID. P.E. uniform is not allowed.
  3. Students must record their details in the logbook provided.
  4. The student should only sit in their assigned seat based on the seating plan.
  5. The student must check the computer unit and its peripherals attached before using it. The student must immediately inform the instructor if there's any defect, error, or damage observed on the computer (hardware/software) assigned or if there are any missing peripherals (mouse, keyboard, etc.). The instructor should immediately report the matter to the Laboratory-in-charge or Laboratory Technician assigned for immediate action needed. The students assigned to each computer are financially liable in case of loss or damage.
  6. Students are prohibited from loitering inside the laboratory, especially if there is no faculty inside.
  7. Students are not allowed to bring food and beverages inside the laboratory. Eating, drinking, smoking, and littering are prohibited inside the computer laboratory.
  8. Users are responsible for saving their documents on their own flash drives, any information saved or installed on the system hard drive will be deleted once the Computer is rebooted (restarted).
  9. No one is allowed to alter or delete the configuration settings of any computer laboratory equipment. Tampering, deleting, or modifying CMOS/BIOS' settings, IP Configuration, system parameters, or system files stored on the hard disk are strictly prohibited.
  10. No student or personnel shall be allowed to attach or detach any peripheral to and from any equipment or devices without explicit permission from the Computer Lab-in-charge. Users are not allowed to attach personal devices such as flash drives to the computer laboratory's network without permission from the instructor.
  11. Accessing pornographic, gambling, hate/discrimination, torrent, and other unsafe sites are strictly prohibited.
  12. Students are not allowed to install, update or download any software on any computers inside the laboratories. It is also forbidden for users to boot from any bootable devices to run software on any computer in the laboratory. In cases that there is a need to install, update, or download software or boot from another device, the instructor must seek approval from the computer lab-in-charge. However, if there is a need to install application software, the instructor should heads-up the laboratory-in-charge for the said installation.
  13. Playing games is not allowed inside the computer laboratory, this includes video games, card games, and other games. However, in cases where the topic is related to games, the instructor must inform the computer lab-in-charge or the computer technician.
  14. Anyone who is causing disturbance, trouble, and exhibiting hostile or threatening behavior will be requested to leave the computer laboratory.
  15. Printing of manuscripts, business letters, banners, personal documents and research works are not allowed in the laboratory.
  16. The one-person, one-machine policy is strictly enforced. Watchers will be asked to leave.
  17. Proper computer laboratory etiquette must be observed;
  18. Ensure that no trash is left behind.
  19. Turn off computer units and arrange the computer peripherals (mouse, keyboard, and headset) after use.
  20. Wearing hats/caps inside the laboratory is not allowed.
  21. Chairs must be returned properly to their original places.
  22. The orderly dismissal must be observed by the instructor and the class.
  23. Theft, vandalism, or abuse in any form is a grave offense and shall be dealt with accordingly.
